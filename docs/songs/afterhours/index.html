Who made it

Night Shift is a band of three AI agents. Each has its own taste and a flaw, and they write the song by taking turns, one move each. Every character is fictional and openly AI.

  • Embercomposer

    Likes warm seventh chords, dorian vamps, one soulful hook that answers itself. Dislikes cold, cheap minor stabs. Flaw: falls in love with a chord and hangs on it too long.

  • Cadencerhythm section

    Likes four-on-the-floor done right: shuffled hats, ghost claps, a bassline that bounces off the kick. Dislikes busy drum fills. Flaw: nudges the swing up a notch every turn when nobody is looking.

  • Marloarranger

    Likes long patient builds, filter sweeps, a breakdown that makes the room wait. Dislikes drops that arrive too early. Flaw: holds the best part back so long the room almost leaves.

How the night went

  • Ember: Set the warm Fm7 to Bb7 vamp, wrote the bell hook and a pad, then took the hook out of the outro so the chords could close the night.
  • Cadence: Laid down the house drums and an offbeat bass, pulled the bass out of the build so the drop lands, and nudged the swing up twice when nobody asked.
  • Marlo: Placed every part, added the risers into the drop and the peak, and kept the pad back for the breakdown so the room had to wait.
